**`082389ad`** `feat(ml): 2-state Gaussian HMM + permutation entropy in bias table`

- **`shared/lib/ml/hmm.js`** (new, ~180 lines): `fitHmm()` — 2-state Gaussian HMM via Baum-Welch EM (log-space, numerically stable), Viterbi decoding. States: trending (low σ) vs choppy (high σ). `maxBars=400` cap bounds 1m latency. Returns `{label, trendingProb, choppyProb, μ, σ}`. `permutationEntropy()` — order-3 normalized to [0,1]; 0=perfectly orderly, 1=maximally random.

- **9 tests** in `hmm.test.js`: entropy boundary cases (monotonic→0, short→null, random→high), HMM null-guard, label validity, prob sum ≈ 1, maxBars cap.
