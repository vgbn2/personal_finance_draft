### 1. Cluster NVMe Write Attribution & Root Cause Isolation
- **Cumulative Storage Footprint Analysis**:
  - Out of 17 active containers across cluster hosts `hpdesk` (Micron NVMe) and `delloptiplex` (Samsung NVMe), 98% of all disk write I/O traced to two specific processes:
    - **CT 112 & CT 123 (9Router)**: 355.6 GB (hpdesk, 82%) and 344.2 GB (delloptiplex, 96%) written by `PID 1783 /sbin/init & systemd-journald` due to uncapped stdout streaming token and HTTP request logging to persistent NVMe journals.
    - **CT 114 (Sovereign Trading Console)**: 69.1 GB (hpdesk, 16%) written by `PID 4158 sovereign_cli.js backfill-daemon` from continuous `.tmp` status and heartbeat JSON file replacement during multi-symbol polling loops.
- **Remediation Plan for 9Router (CT 112 / CT 123)**:
  - Formulated systemd-journald volatile logging configuration (`Storage=volatile`, `RuntimeMaxUse=64M`) in `/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/volatile.conf` redirecting all log buffers to RAM tmpfs (`/run/log/journal`), eliminating NVMe token churn.
  - Recommended routing 9Router service stdout to `/dev/null` or setting `StandardOutput=null` for high-token SSE streaming endpoints.
