## 1. Fed Rates (`fed_rates/`)
**Engineering Domain:** Control Systems & Estimation Theory

### 📐 The Math
We model the Federal Reserve as a **PID Controller** minimizing the error between inflation ($i(t)$) and the 2% target ($i_{target}$).
$$u(t) = K_p e(t) + K_i \int e(t) dt + K_d \frac{de(t)}{dt}$$
Where $e(t) = i(t) - i_{target}$.

A **Kalman Filter** is then applied to separate the "true" policy signal ($x_k$) from market noise ($v_k$).
$$z_k = H x_k + v_k$$
The filter recursively estimates the state $x_k$ (the actual probability/rate) by weighing the model prediction against the noisy market observation $z_k$.

### ⚠️ When It Fails
- **Regime Change**: If the Fed abandons the Taylor Rule (e.g., during a financial crisis or war), the PID gains ($K_p, K_i, K_d$) become invalid.
- **Data Lag**: The model relies on CPI/PCE data which looks backward. Real-time shocks (e.g., bank failures) are invisible to the PID until data updates.

### 🎯 Positioning Strategy
- **LONG HAWKISH**: When PID Signal > Market Implied Rate + 20bps (and inflation is accelerating).
- **LONG DOVISH**: When PID Signal < Market Implied Rate - 20bps (and unemployment spikes).
- **STAY AWAY**: When the Kalman Noise Estimate ($\pm \sigma$) is high (>50bps), indicating the market is pricing in an unknown binary event.

## 2. BTC Price (`btc_price/`)
**Engineering Domain:** Digital Signal Processing (DSP)

### 📐 The Math
We treat the BTC price action as a digital signal $x[n]$ and apply the **Z-Transform**:
$$X(z) = \sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty} x[n] z^{-n}$$
We fit an AR (Autoregressive) model to find the **Poles** ($p_k$) of the system transfer function $H(z)$.
- **Stability**: A system is stable if all poles lie within the unit circle ($|p_k| < 1$).
- **Instability/Reversal**: As a pole approaches the unit circle ($|p_k| \to 1$), the system nears a state of oscillation or exponential growth/decay (breakout/reversal).

### ⚠️ When It Fails
- **Exogenous Shocks**: The Z-transform assumes the signal is generated by a linear difference equation. It cannot predict meaningful regulatory news (e.g., SEC approval) or hacks.
- **Low Liquidity**: In thin markets, price is noise-dominated, leading to "false poles" that don't represent true trend dynamics.

### 🎯 Positioning Strategy
- **BUY BREAKOUT**: When Stability Metric > 0.95 (Pole near unit circle) AND Trend is clearly Bullish.
- **MEAN REVERSION**: When Stability Metric < 0.80 (Deep inside unit circle) AND price is > 2$\sigma$ from moving average.
- **CLUMPED ARBITRAGE**: When the sum of implied probabilities for a contiguous range (e.g., \$90k-\$100k) is < fair value, use **Dutch Betting** to lock in equal payout across the clump.

## 3. SpaceX Launches (`spacex_launches/`)
**Engineering Domain:** Reliability Engineering

### 📐 The Math
We model launch success as a **Bayesian Reliability Function** updated by **Impulse Responses**.
$$P(S|E) = \frac{P(E|S) \cdot P(S)}{P(E)}$$
Each successful milestone (Static Fire, Wet Dress Rehearsal) is an "impulse" that shifts the prior probability $P(S)$ upward via a Logit transform:
$$logit(p_{new}) = logit(p_{old}) + w_{impulse}$$

### ⚠️ When It Fails
- **Hidden Failure Modes**: A "successful" Static Fire might actually reveal a micro-fracture that isn't public knowledge. The model assumes public signals correlate 1:1 with engineering health.
- **Go Fever**: The model assumes rational actors. If political pressure forces a launch despite failed milestones, the Bayesian update will underestimate risk.

### 🎯 Positioning Strategy
- **LONG SUCCESS**: Buy immediately *after* a successful major milestone (e.g., Static Fire) if the market reacts slowly (impulse lag).
- **SHORT/HEDGE**: If a milestone is delayed repeatedly (time decay of reliability) but market price remains sticky/high.
- **EXIT**: Sell 24-48h before T-0 to avoid the binary "coin flip" of the actual launch, capturing the "hype run-up" value.

## 4. Hurricane Markets (`hurricane_markets/`)
**Engineering Domain:** Stochastic Processes

### 📐 The Math
Hurricane intensity is modeled as a **Discrete-Time Markov Chain**.
State Space $S = \{TD, TS, Cat1, Cat2, Cat3, Cat4, Cat5\}$
Transition Matrix $T$ is dynamic, modulated by environmental inputs:
$$T_{ij}(SST, Shear) = T_{base} + \delta(SST) - \delta(Shear)$$
We quantify **Spectral Leakage** as the probability mass that the market incorrectly assigns to high-energy states (Cat 5) due to "lottery ticket" bias.

### ⚠️ When It Fails
- **Rapid Intensification (RI)**: Physics-based RI is highly non-linear and often defies Markovian "memoryless" assumptions.
- **Land Interaction**: The model struggles when a storm skims a coast; land interaction degrades structure in complex ways not captured by simple SST/Shear variables.

### 🎯 Positioning Strategy
- **FADE EXTREMES**: Sell "Cat 5" outcomes when Spectral Leakage is detected (Model Prob < 1% vs Market > 5%).
- **BUY CLUMPS**: Buy the "likely center" (e.g., Cat 2 + Cat 3) when uncertainty is high, effectively shorting the tails (volatility selling).

## 5. Internet Outages (`internet_outages/`)
**Engineering Domain:** Reliability & Statistical Quality Control

### 📐 The Math
We treat global uptime as a steady state and outages as Poisson processes.
**Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)**: $\theta = \frac{T}{R}$
Failure Rate: $\lambda = \frac{1}{\theta}$
Probability of outage in window $t$:
$$P(N \ge 1) = 1 - e^{-\lambda t}$$
We calculate a **Fair Value** confidence interval using the Beta distribution for small sample sizes.

### ⚠️ When It Fails
- **Clustered Failures**: Outages are often correlated (e.g., a bad CrowdStrike update hits Azure, AWS, and GCP simultaneously). Poisson assumes independence.
- **Black Swans**: A cyber-warfare event or undersea cable cut is a structural break, not a statistical fluctuation.

### 🎯 Positioning Strategy
- **SHORT OUTAGE (Long Uptime)**: When Market Price > Fair Value + 2$\sigma$. This is "picking up pennies in front of a steamroller," but statistically profitable.
- **LONG OUTAGE**: Only valid when λ is structurally increasing (e.g., active solar flare cycle or known zero-day vulnerability period).
