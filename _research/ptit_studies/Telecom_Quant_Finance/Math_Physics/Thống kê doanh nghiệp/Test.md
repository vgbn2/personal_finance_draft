## Part I: Multiple Choice Concept Check

- **Question 1:** **What is the primary focus of "Thống kê học"?**
   A.  The detailed analysis of individual company finances.
   B.  The study of methods for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data.
   C.  The creation of marketing strategies based on market trends.
   D.  The legal aspects of data privacy and security.

   <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
   <p><b>B. The study of methods for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data.</b></p>
   <p>“Thống kê học” directly translates to “Statistics,” emphasizing the systematic process of data analysis and interpretation. Options A, C, and D are related to other fields, not the core definition of statistics.</p>
   </details>

- **Question 2:** **Which of the following best describes the “Tổng thể thống kê”?**
   A.  A specific product manufactured by a company.
   B.  The entire group of objects that we want to study.
   C.  A detailed financial report of a business.
   D.  A marketing campaign targeting a specific demographic.

   <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
   <p><b>B. The entire group of objects that we want to study.</b></p>
   <p>The “Tổng thể thống kê” represents the population from which a sample is drawn, enabling statistical inference. Options A, C, and D are more specific and related to business operations.</p>
   </details>

- **Question 3:** **The "Hồi quy tuyến tính" method is primarily used for:**
    A.  Calculating the average value of a dataset.
    B.  Modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables, assuming a linear relationship.
    C.  Creating visualizations of data distributions.
    D.  Performing complex mathematical calculations.

   <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
   <p><b>B. Modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables, assuming a linear relationship.</b></p>
   <p>“Hồi quy tuyến tính” literally translates to “linear regression,” which is a statistical technique specifically designed to model linear relationships between variables.</p>
   </details>

- **Question 4:** **What is the main purpose of calculating a “Chỉ số đơn”?**
   A.  To analyze complex relationships between multiple variables.
   B.  To measure a specific aspect of development or performance using a single variable.
   C.  To create a comprehensive overview of a business's overall performance.
   D.  To predict future trends based on historical data.

   <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
   <p><b>B. To measure a specific aspect of development or performance using a single variable.</b></p>
   <p>“Chỉ số đơn” is designed to isolate and quantify a particular element of a trend or measure, often used to simplify analysis.</p>
   </details>

- **Question 5:** **The concept of “Giá trị gia tăng thuần của doanh nghiệp” represents:**
   A.  The total cost of goods sold.
   B.  The difference between output value and intermediate input costs.
   C.  The revenue generated by a company's products or services.
   D.  The expenses incurred by a company during its operations.

   <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
   <p><b>B. The difference between output value and intermediate input costs.</b></p>
   <p>“Giá trị gia tăng thuần” is defined as the value created by a business, reflecting the difference between the value of the output and the value of the inputs used to produce that output.</p>
   </details>

## Part II: Formula Application & Short Answer

- **Question 1:** **A company wants to analyze the relationship between advertising expenditure (independent variable) and sales revenue (dependent variable). They use a linear regression model.  Explain in a short paragraph how the “Hồi quy tuyến tính” formula would be applied in this scenario, including the key assumptions.**

   <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
   <p>In this scenario, the “Hồi quy tuyến tính” formula (typically represented as y = mx + b) would be used to estimate the relationship between advertising expenditure (x) and sales revenue (y). The formula would be derived based on the data collected, and the slope (m) and intercept (b) would be calculated. A key assumption is that the relationship between advertising and sales revenue is linear. The model’s effectiveness depends on the validity of this assumption, as well as the absence of outliers significantly affecting the regression line.</p>
   </details>

- **Question 2:** **A manufacturing company tracks its production output (in units) over a five-year period.  The company wants to calculate the “Số tương đối động thái” to assess the change in production efficiency. Provide the formula and briefly explain how it would be used to determine the percentage change in production output from the initial year to the final year.**

   <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
   <p>The formula for “Số tương đối động thái” (tđt) is:  `tđt = (y1 / y0)`.  Where y1 is the production output in the final year and y0 is the production output in the initial year.  This formula calculates the ratio of the final output to the initial output, expressed as a decimal.  Multiplying this decimal by 100 will then convert it into a percentage, representing the relative change in production output over the five-year period. For example, if y1 = 1000 and y0 = 800, then tđt = 1.25, representing a 25% increase in production output.</p>
   </details>

- **Question 3:** **A retail company calculates its “Chi phí trung gian của doanh nghiệp” for each product sold. They then use this data to calculate the “Giá trị gia tăng thuần” for each product.  Explain how the two concepts are related and why understanding both is important for business decision-making.**

   <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
   <p>“Chi phí trung gian” represents the cost of materials and components used to produce a product, while “Giá trị gia tăng thuần” is the difference between the revenue generated by selling that product and its associated “chi phí trung gian”. The two concepts are fundamentally linked through the value chain. Understanding both is crucial for businesses to accurately assess profitability, optimize production costs, and make informed decisions regarding pricing, sourcing, and product development.  A high “Giá trị gia tăng thuần” indicates a strong value proposition, while a low “chi phí trung gian” contributes to enhanced profitability.</p>
   </details>
