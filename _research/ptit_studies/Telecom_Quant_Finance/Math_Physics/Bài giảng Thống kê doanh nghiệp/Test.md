## Part I: Multiple Choice Concept Check

- **Question 1:**
  > What is the primary definition of “Thống kê” in the context of a statistical study?
  >
  > A.  A collection of all possible outcomes.
  > B.  The process of analyzing data for patterns.
  > C.  A statistical population, referring to all elements of interest in a statistical study.
  > D.  The application of mathematical models to business data.
  <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
  <p>C. A statistical population, referring to all elements of interest in a statistical study.</p>
  <p>This definition aligns with the fundamental concept of statistics – examining a group of elements to draw conclusions about a larger population.</p>
  </details>

- **Question 2:**
  > Which of the following best describes the purpose of “Điều tra chọn mẫu”?
  >
  > A.  To conduct a comprehensive survey of the entire population.
  > B.  To systematically collect data from a subset of a larger group to represent it.
  > C.  To analyze data using statistical software.
  > D.  To develop new statistical models.
  <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
  <p>B. To systematically collect data from a subset of a larger group to represent it.</p>
  <p>Sampling is a crucial technique in statistics, allowing for efficient data collection and inference about a population.</p>
  </details>

## Part II: Formula Application & Short Answer

- **Question 1:**
  > A company is tracking its monthly sales revenue. The data for the past 5 months is as follows: $10,000, $12,000, $11,500, $13,000, $12,800.  Calculate the Simple Moving Average (SMA) over a period of 3 months.
  >
  > *Use the formula: SMA = (Σxᵢ) / n*
  <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
  <p>1.  Calculate the sum of the last 3 months' data: $11,500 + $12,800 + $13,000 = $37,300</p>
  <p>2.  Divide the sum by the number of periods (3): $37,300 / 3 = $12,433.33</p>
  <p>Therefore, the SMA over 3 months is $12,433.33</p>
  </details>

- **Question 2:**
  > A finance analyst is studying the trend in a company's revenue over several years.  The company’s revenue for the years 2020, 2021, and 2022 is $5,000,000, $5,500,000, and $6,000,000 respectively. Calculate the percentage change in revenue from 2020 to 2021.
  >
  > *Use the formula: Percentage Change = (y1 - y0) / y0*
  <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
  <p>1.  Identify the initial and final values: y1 = $5,500,000, y0 = $5,000,000</p>
  <p>2.  Calculate the difference: $5,500,000 - $5,000,000 = $500,000</p>
  <p>3.  Calculate the percentage change: ($500,000 / $5,000,000) * 100% = 10%</p>
  <p>Therefore, the percentage change in revenue from 2020 to 2021 is 10%.</p>
  </details>

- **Question 3:**
  > A manufacturing company wants to assess the impact of a new marketing campaign on sales. They collect data on sales volume (in units) and the amount spent on the campaign (in dollars) for a period of 6 months.  Given the data:
  >
  > | Month | Sales Volume (Units) | Campaign Cost ($) |
  > |---|---|---|
  > | 1 | 100 | 10,000 |
  > | 2 | 110 | 10,000 |
  > | 3 | 125 | 10,000 |
  > | 4 | 140 | 10,000 |
  > | 5 | 155 | 10,000 |
  > | 6 | 170 | 10,000 |
  >
  > Using the formula for the *Công thức tính số bình quân điều hoà*, calculate the average sales volume over the 6 months.
  > *Use the formula: x̅ = √(∑(xi^2 * fi) / ∑fi)*
  <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
  <p>1. Calculate the sum of xi^2 * fi for each month:</p>
  <p>Month 1: (100)^2 * 1 = 10000</p>
  <p>Month 2: (110)^2 * 1 = 12100</p>
  <p>Month 3: (125)^2 * 1 = 15625</p>
  <p>Month 4: (140)^2 * 1 = 19600</p>
  <p>Month 5: (155)^2 * 1 = 24025</p>
  <p>Month 6: (170)^2 * 1 = 28900</p>
  <p>2. Calculate the sum of (xi^2 * fi): 10000 + 12100 + 15625 + 19600 + 24025 + 28900 = 110250</p>
  <p>3. Calculate the sum of fi: 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 6</p>
  <p>4. Calculate the value of the square root: √ (110250 / 6) = √(18375) ≈ 135.60</p>
  <p>Therefore, the average sales volume over the 6 months is approximately 135.60 units.</p>
  </details>
