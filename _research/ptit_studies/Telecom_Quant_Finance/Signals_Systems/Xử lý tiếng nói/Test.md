## Part I: Multiple Choice Concept Check

- **Question 1:**
  > What is the primary purpose of the Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT)?
  >
  > A.  To analyze signals in the time domain.
  > B.  To convert a signal from the time domain to the frequency domain.
  > C.  To remove noise from audio signals.
  > D.  To measure the amplitude of sound waves.
  >
  <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
  <p>B. To convert a signal from the time domain to the frequency domain.</p>
  <p>The DFT is a fundamental transformation that decomposes a signal into its constituent frequencies, effectively shifting the representation from the time domain to the frequency domain. </p>
  </details>

- **Question 2:**
  > Which of the following best describes the process of ‘Xử lý tiếng nói’?
  >
  > A.  The physical act of speaking.
  > B.  The conversion of audio information into a digital format for processing and back again.
  > C.  The creation of musical instruments.
  > D.  The recording of sound waves.
  >
  <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
  <p>B. The conversion of audio information into a digital format for processing and back again.</p>
  <p>Xử lý tiếng nói encompasses the transformation of speech from an analog signal into a digital representation for manipulation and the subsequent conversion back to an audible form.</p>
  </details>

- **Question 3:**
  > What is the main function of the ‘Bộ não’ in speech processing?
  >
  > A.  To amplify the sound waves.
  > B.  To analyze the message and control the articulatory muscles.
  > C.  To record the speech signal.
  > D.  To filter out background noise.
  >
  <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
  <p>B. To analyze the message and control the articulatory muscles.</p>
  <p>The ‘Bộ não’ is responsible for the cognitive aspects of speech, including message interpretation and coordinating the muscles involved in speech production.</p>
  </details>

## Part II: Formula Application & Short Answer

- **Question 1:**
  > A sound engineer measures the root mean square (rms) pressure of a speech signal to be 0.1 Pa, and the reference pressure is 1 Pa. Calculate the Sound Pressure Level (SPL) of the speech signal using the given formula:  `SPL = 20 * log10(rmsP/P0)`.  Show your calculations.

  <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
  <p>1.  Calculate the ratio: rmsP/P0 = 0.1 Pa / 1 Pa = 0.1</p>
  <p>2.  Apply the formula: SPL = 20 * log10(0.1) = 20 * (-1) = -20 dB</p>
  <p>Therefore, the SPL of the speech signal is -20 dB.</p>
  </details>

- **Question 2:**
  >  You are analyzing a recording of a speech signal.  The signal intensity (I) is measured to be 1000 µPa, and the reference intensity (I0) is 1 µPa.  Calculate the Sound Intensity Level (SIL) using the formula: `SIL = 10 * log10(I/I0)`.  Show your calculations and state the units of the SIL.

  <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
  <p>1. Calculate the ratio: I/I0 = 1000 µPa / 1 µPa = 1000</p>
  <p>2. Apply the formula: SIL = 10 * log10(1000) = 10 * 3 = 30 dB</p>
  <p>Therefore, the Sound Intensity Level (SIL) is 30 dB.</p>
  </details>

- **Question 3:**
  > Using the formula `Ngƣỡng nghe tuyệt đối: 43.31000/f(6.08.0)`, estimate the absolute hearing threshold (TOH) for a person with a frequency of 1000 Hz. Show your calculations and state the units of the TOH.

  <details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
  <p>1. Substitute f = 1000 Hz into the formula: TOH = 43.31000 / (6.08 * 0.008) = 43.31000 / 0.04864 = 899.99 Hz</p>
  <p>Therefore, the estimated absolute hearing threshold (TOH) for a person with a frequency of 1000 Hz is approximately 900 Hz.</p>
  </details>
