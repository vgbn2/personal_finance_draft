**2. According to Locke's Goal-Setting Theory, what is the primary impact of setting specific and challenging goals?**
   > A.  Decreased employee motivation and engagement.
   > B.  Lower overall organizational performance.
   > C.  Higher performance levels compared to less specific goals.
   > D.  Increased levels of confusion and uncertainty among employees.

   <details><summary>Reveal Answer</summary>
   <pre>
   **C. Higher performance levels compared to less specific goals.**
   </pre>

**1. Scenario:** A company is launching a new marketing campaign and wants to ensure the messaging is easily understood by its target audience. The company uses the Flesch Reading Ease score to assess the clarity of the campaign materials. The score is 65.  What does this score indicate, and what action should the company take?

<details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
<pre>
**Explanation:** The Flesch Reading Ease score measures the readability of text. A score of 65 indicates that the text is relatively difficult to read.  This suggests the language used is too complex for the target audience.

**Action:** The company should simplify the language, use shorter sentences, and avoid jargon to improve the readability score and ensure effective communication.
</pre>
</details>

**2. Scenario:**  A company is evaluating its long-term sustainability and wants to understand the impact of its operations on the environment and its financial investment. They use the Sustainability Assessment formula: Sustainability = (Financial Investment) - (Environmental Impact).  The Financial Investment is $5 million and the Environmental Impact is $1.5 million. Calculate the Sustainability score.

<details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
<pre>
**Calculation:** Sustainability = $5,000,000 - $1,500,000 = $3,500,000

**Interpretation:** The Sustainability score of $3,500,000 indicates a positive contribution to sustainability, as the financial investment exceeds the environmental impact.
</pre>
</details>


**3. Scenario:** A manager wants to improve the effectiveness of team meetings. They apply the Communication Gap formula: Gap = (Lack of Information) / (Potential for Understanding).  The Lack of Information is identified as a significant misunderstanding of project goals, and the Potential for Understanding is high due to a well-trained team. Calculate the Gap.

<details><summary>Reveal Solution</summary>
<pre>
**Assumption:** Let’s assume the Lack of Information is quantified as a 70% misunderstanding of the project goals.  The Potential for Understanding is considered 100% (fully trained team).

**Calculation:** Gap = 70% / 100% = 0.7

**Interpretation:** A Gap of 0.7 indicates a significant communication deficiency – a substantial portion of the team still doesn’t fully understand the project goals.  The manager should focus on clarifying goals and providing more detailed instructions.
</pre>
</details>
